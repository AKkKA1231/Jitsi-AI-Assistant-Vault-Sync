Jitsi AI Assistant Vault & Sync

1-Page Team Quickstart Guide • Browser Extension Setup

Chrome • Edge • Brave
1

Download & Extract

  1. Download the project from GitHub:
    github.com/AKkKA1231/Jitsi-AI-Assistant-Vault-Sync
  2. Click Code > Download ZIP.
  3. Extract the ZIP folder onto your computer.
  4. Locate the extension/ folder inside.
2

Load in Browser

  1. Open your browser's extension page:
    • Chrome: chrome://extensions
    • Edge: edge://extensions
  2. Turn ON the Developer mode toggle (top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked (top-left).
  4. Select the extension/ folder.
3

Run in Jitsi Meet

  1. Join any meeting:
    meet.jit.si/YourCallRoom
  2. Click the floating AI Assistant button on the right edge.
  3. Click Settings (⚙️) and paste your free Gemini API Key (aistudio.google.com).
  4. Click Start Recording to capture & sync!

🛡️ Zero-Loss IndexedDB Audio Vault (Crash & Disconnect Protection)

If your Wi-Fi drops or the laptop battery sleeps mid-meeting, call audio is not lost! Simply rejoin the room. A yellow "Meeting Interruption Detected" banner will offer an immediate 1-click "Restore Recording" button.

Fault Tolerant
Floating Widget Not Appearing?

Verify you are on a meet.jit.si URL and press F5 (refresh) once after loading the unpacked extension.

Microphone Permission

Ensure your browser has permitted microphone access to the Jitsi tab so the Web Audio mixer can capture both speaker and mic audio.

Where are Transcripts Saved?

Transcripts and executive summaries are rendered directly in the sidebar, downloadable as Markdown, and synced to Google Drive if configured.

Updating to Newer Versions

Pull or download the latest repo code, open chrome://extensions, and click the circular Reload (🔄) button on the extension card.
